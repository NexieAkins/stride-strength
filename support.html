Stride & Strength Support

Stride & Strength helps you plan workouts, track runs and meals, and see your progress — on iPhone, Android and the web.

Contact us: support@stridenstrength.com
We aim to reply within 2 business days. Please include your device (iPhone/Android/web) and, if you can, a screenshot.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. You can log workouts, runs, meals, water, weight and your fitness profile as a guest — it's saved on your device. A free account adds cloud backup and sync across devices, AI meal estimates and plans, private progress photos and Apple Health sync. Anything you logged as a guest moves into your account when you sign up.

I didn't get my verification or password-reset email

Check your spam or junk folder. Emails come from our sign-in provider. You can request a new password-reset link from the sign-in screen with “Forgot password”. Still stuck? Email us.

How do I sync between my phone and the website?

Sign in with the same account on each device. Your workouts, meals, weigh-ins, water and goals sync automatically.

Apple Health and Apple Watch

On iPhone, you can allow Stride & Strength to read and save workouts in Apple Health so your workouts appear in both places without duplicates. You can change this any time in the iPhone Settings app under Health → Data Access & Devices.

Are my progress photos private?

Yes. Progress photos are stored in a private, per-user folder, encrypted at rest, and only your signed-in account can view them. For extra protection, turn on Private Vault in My Transformation: photos are encrypted on your device before upload so not even we can see them. If you forget your vault passcode and lose your recovery code, vault photos can't be recovered by anyone.

Are the nutrition and calorie numbers exact?

No — they're estimates. Packaged foods use product label data where available; meals are estimated. Stride & Strength is not medical advice; talk to a qualified professional about your health, diet or training.

How do I delete my account and data?

Sign in, open the account menu → Account & sync → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, cloud records and private photos. See our Privacy Policy for details.
