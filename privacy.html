Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Who we are

Stride & Strength is operated by Olufunto (individual developer). Contact: support@stridenstrength.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password (stored securely by our authentication provider, Supabase).
  • Fitness and nutrition records you enter: workouts, runs, meals, weigh-ins, water logs, goals and profile details such as height, age and training preferences.
  • Sensitive profile information: if you choose to enter them, your pregnancy or breastfeeding status (used to set nutrition targets) and your cuisine preferences (used to suggest meals). This information is used only to personalize the app for you.
  • Private progress photos: photos you choose to upload to My Transformation. These are stored privately, and only your signed-in account can view them.
  • Photos you send for analysis: meal, equipment and run photos you submit for AI estimates are sent to our AI provider to produce the result.
  • Browser data: preferences and unsaved drafts are kept on your device.

How we use it

We use your information to provide the app: to sync your records across devices, generate estimates and plans you request, and show your progress. We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for advertising.

Service providers

  • Supabase: account authentication, the database and private photo storage.
  • OpenAI: processes the meal, equipment, run and plan requests you submit. Content sent to the AI service is used to produce your result.
  • Netlify: hosts the app and runs its server functions.

Health information

Stride & Strength is a tracking tool, not a medical service. Estimates and plans are approximate and are not medical advice. Talk with a qualified professional before starting a new exercise or nutrition plan.

Your choices

  • Export: download a backup of your records from the Progress section.
  • Delete: you can delete your account and its cloud records and photos from the Cloud account section of the app. See the section below.

Deleting your account

Sign in, open the Cloud account section, and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your account, your cloud records and your private photos. Records saved only on your device stay on that device until you clear the browser’s data. If you cannot sign in, email support@stridenstrength.com and we will help.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
