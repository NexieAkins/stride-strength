How to use Stride & Strength

🚀 Getting started

  • Guest: no sign-up needed. You can log workouts, runs, meals, water and weight — all saved on this phone.
  • Free account: needed for AI estimates and plans, progress photos and syncing between devices.
  • Anything you logged as a guest moves into your account when you sign up.
  • Tap the person icon at the top right to sign in, or to open your menu.

🏠 Home

  • See today’s workout, calories left, water and your week’s rings at a glance.
  • Tap a card to jump straight to that part of the app.

🏋️ Planning & doing a workout

  • Plan: open Train, pick the type of workout and let the planner build it.
  • Start: tap Start to open your session.
  • Tick sets: tap a set’s circle when you finish it. The rest timer starts on its own.
  • Plank timer & Max hold: for timed moves, start the timer. Tap ∞ Max hold to time your longest hold and log it.
  • Copy last set: repeats your previous weight and reps in one tap.
  • Pause — finish later saves your place. Finish & log saves the workout to your history.
  • Repeat a workout: pick a past session and start it again.
  • Left something unfinished? A Log it card appears so you can save what you did.

🏃 Runs & walks

  • Outdoors: start a run or walk and allow location. GPS tracks distance and pace.
  • Treadmill: start the timer, then enter the distance when you finish.

🍽️ Logging meals

  • Photo: snap your plate (free account).
  • Scan barcode: point the camera at a packaged food.
  • Type or say: describe it, or tap the mic and speak.
  • Enter it: type calories and protein yourself.
  • Recent: re-add something you ate before.
  • Estimate nutrition fills in the numbers for you. They are estimates, not exact.
  • Had another helping? Tap ＋1 serving.
  • Log again copies a past meal to today.
  • Search Past days to find any earlier meal.

🥘 Kitchen & Menus

  • Kitchen: list what you have and get meal ideas that fit your goals.
  • Menus: build a day of meals, then add them to your plan.

💧 Water

  • Tap the water buttons on Home or Meals to add a glass or bottle.
  • Your water ring fills as you go.

🎯 Goals

  • Edit goal: change your calorie and protein targets any time.
  • Weekend flex: give weekends a different target without changing weekdays.

📈 Progress

  • Browse your workout and meal history. Use the 🔍 box to search it.
  • Add weigh-ins to see your weight trend.
  • Check best lifts and earn achievements as you stay consistent.

📸 Progress photos & Private Vault

  • Add photos in My Transformation to compare over time. They are private to your account.
  • Turn on Private Vault to encrypt photos on your phone before upload. You set a passcode and get a recovery code.
Important: if you forget your passcode and lose your recovery code, vault photos cannot be recovered by anyone. Save the code somewhere safe.

⌚ Apple Health & Watch (iPhone)

  • Allow Stride & Strength to read and save workouts in Apple Health.
  • Workouts from your Apple Watch show up without duplicates.
  • Change permissions any time in iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

⚙️ Settings

  • Tap the person icon, then Aa Text size & display for larger text and skins.
  • Fitness preferences hold your weekly schedule, meal style and diet rules.
  • Edit your fitness profile (age, weight, goals) from preferences.

💬 Help & contact
